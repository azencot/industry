Conversations with **industry research groups** (open-ended ML research inside a company lab), as distinct from a posted applied-scientist or product loop.

**Not** Health AIML or other product seats ([`GenAI/interviews/apple-health-aiml/`](../GenAI/interviews/apple-health-aiml/)). **Not** scientific-ML / physical-AI loops ([`ScientificML/`](../ScientificML/)). **Not** forecasting ([`Forecasting/`](../Forecasting/)). **Not** FinTelligence ([`Amazon_FinTech/`](../Amazon_FinTech/)).
