## Role in one line

End-to-end applied scientist for **forecasting, pacing, and recommendation** systems that plan and allocate spend on **emerging channels** (CTV, podcasts, live events, …) — custom models, not off-the-shelf, shipped with engineering into always-on production.

## Public product context (don’t overclaim internals)

TTD is the independent DSP for the open internet. Kokai (their buying platform) already sells **campaign forecasting** (reach / spend / delivery before go-live) and **channel recommendations** (suggest another channel, one-click new forecast). Related public pieces:

- [Plan campaigns with forecasting](https://www.thetradedesk.com/resources/plan-campaigns-with-confidence-using-forecasting)
- [Channel forecasting + channel recommendations](https://www.thetradedesk.com/resources/kokai-platform-experience-update-channel-recommendations)
- [Live Events forecasting / pacing](https://www.thetradedesk.com/resources/forecast-plan-activate-live-sports-with-live-events-kokai)

**Decision Power Score** (0–100; ~80+ = enough inventory scale to optimize) is a public forecast KPI — useful vocabulary, not something to lecture on.

This Channel Growth req is **emerging-channel** forecasting + **pacing** + recs. Asking how the two teams split is a good recruiter/HM question.
