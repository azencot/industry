# Prep — FinTelligence hiring-manager call (L5 inclination)

**Status:** Satya (recruiter) said the team is **considering an L5 offer** on the Senior Applied Scientist loop. Next step she described: a **phone call with the hiring manager**, then an offer. **Not scheduled yet.** Get the name, length, and format from her before you prep past this page.

**Your goal on the call:** leave the manager able to say *he wants the IC job, he understands L5 scope, he will not show up bitter.* Level and money stay with Satya.

Hub: [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md). PS1 record: [`debrief/2026-06-30_ps1-karan-real-interview.md`](debrief/2026-06-30_ps1-karan-real-interview.md).

## 0. Before you accept the slot (email Satya)

Ask these in one note. The answers change the prep.

1. Who is the hiring manager (name and title)? Is this Karan or someone else?
2. How long, and is it a conversation about the role or another interview?
3. Is L5 the debrief outcome, or still one option among others?
4. What title would be on an L5 offer, and is the req the same team?
5. Location and onsite expectation.
6. Will compensation be discussed on this call, or only with you afterward?

Until she answers (3), treat “considering an offer” as an inclination. A written offer is numbers from Satya, later.

---

## 1. What this call is

| It is | Leave it for Satya |
|-------|--------------------|
| 20–30 min on the job you would actually do | Re-trying the loop (no coding, no LP monologue, no VLM tour) |
| The manager checking you will do hands-on science at L5 and stay | Arguing the debrief or asking them to flip the level |
| Your chance to see year-one scope | Base, RSUs, sign-on, band position |

Amazon sets level in the debrief, with the bar raiser. The hiring manager usually cannot uplevel you on this call. A candidate who spends the call on “I should be L6” gives them a flight-risk signal and no new information.

**Private decision, before you dial:** you can be genuinely interested in the L5 seat if the work is end-to-end science and the written package is a real L5 package. You will not ask this manager for a promo date. You will decide yes or no after the letter.

L5 here means: you are given a real problem, you own the approach, the experiments, the eval, and the ship-or-kill call, mostly inside one team. L6 on the posting meant: you define an ambiguous area and other people adopt your mechanism. Walk in ready to do the first job well.

## 2. Say this (aloud twice)

### Open, ~45 seconds — then stop

I’m Omri. I build the model, the data, and the eval myself — generative and multimodal models on messy sequential data, mostly NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR. The recent stack is a VLM that answers questions about time series. I owned the training runs and the gates. On TSRBench an open 8B landed around 46 percent, just under much larger proprietary systems. When a mix improved the average and dropped the slice it was built to fix, I killed it.

Satya said the team is looking at an L5 offer. I want this conversation to be about what I would own. I’ll work through level and compensation with her.

### If they ask why you are leaving a PI job

The work I want more of is training, evaluation, and the decision to ship or kill. Finance is a place where that decision matters, because precision is a compliance requirement. I am looking for a team where I am in the code and the eval, with scientists and engineers, on systems people use.

### If they ask whether L5 is a problem

I interviewed for the senior posting, so I heard the level from Satya with that in mind. I am interested in the work. I want to understand what I would own in the first year. I will not relitigate the loop here.

### If they ask where else you are interviewing

I have other applied-science conversations. I am serious about this team, and I can move once there is a written offer.

### If they ask for a number

I’d like to see the scope first. I’m comfortable working through the full package with Satya.

### If they pull a technical thread

One layer, then stop. Same rule as the PS1: 90 seconds, one metric, wait.

- What you built: dual-tower VLM, two-stage training, tiered eval, config sweeps. You ran it.
- The kill: slice floors set before training; target task down 5 points; mix not promoted.
- FinTech bridge, one sentence: offline accuracy gets you to shadow; slice-level precision and a false-action gate get you to automation.

Karan already heard the long version. If this call is with him, do not retell it.

## 3. Ask two of these

Keep each to one or two sentences. Let them talk.

1. **Year-one ownership.** What would I own in the first six months, and what does a strong first year look like — a shipped change, an eval gate, or a domain?
2. **Trust.** Where is it still hardest for finance teams to rely on the system without manual review?
3. **Team.** Who would I work with week to week, and what is already in production versus still being built?
4. **Level, once.** What does the next level actually require on this team — scope other people adopt, or a larger personal project? Ask this only after (1). Then stop. No date, no commitment.

Skip culture, “why did I miss L6,” and anything you can read in the posting.

---

## 4. On the call

- Answer, then stop. The PS1 failure mode was length.
- Say “I” and name what you built, trained, measured, or killed.
- Green card only if they ask about start or sponsorship. You do not need sponsorship.
- Write down, in their words: team, your slice, location, who you’d sit with, anything they say L5 does and does not include.
- End with: I’m interested. I’ll follow up with Satya on next steps.

---

## 5. Afterward

Same day, a few lines in a debrief: their questions, what they said you would own, location, any level comment, whether it felt like a sell or a veto.

Then with Satya, only after you have heard the scope:

- Confirm title, level, team, location, start-date range.
- Ask for the L5 band and the target total compensation for this req before you anchor.
- Levers if the base is capped: sign-on and the year-1 equity grant. Competing processes are leverage with her, stated plainly.
- L5 → L6 on an Amazon science team is commonly more than a year and needs L6-shaped evidence collected while you are L5. A verbal “we’ll look at you soon” is not part of the offer.
